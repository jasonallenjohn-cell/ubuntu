# Tollendale Village II — Common Ground package (DRAFT, password-protected)

Illustrative Ubuntu Land Trust / Common Ground Initiative underwriting for
Tollendale Village II, 1870 & 1902 Big Bay Point Road, Innisfil (SP-2020-007,
MZO O. Reg. 251/19): the 588 apartments reported in April 2026 (the MZO permits 388) under the
Common Ground model, plus a Care Campus page covering the 160 LTC beds and 52 special-care
suites with Heal House as proposed operator.

All HTML pages are encrypted with staticrypt (same approach as the Janefield
package) and the workbook is password-protected; the password is held by Jason
and is not stored in this repository. Plaintext working copies live in the
private Drive folder.

Generated September 12, 2026 with the `ubuntu-common-ground` generator from
`config.json`. Physical and market inputs come from the Arterial connector;
see the **Provenance** tab in the workbook for every input, its source and
its status.
