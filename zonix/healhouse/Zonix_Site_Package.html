02 · How the site comes together
  1. LiUNA's pension backs Nesting Ground's guarantee pool, lifting the land loan from $19.5M to the full $31.0M price — an $11.5M guarantee.
  2. Buy from the receiver and pay out the secured lenders; $8.7M of equity at purchase covers transfer tax, carry and pre-development.
  3. Tower A and Tower B close construction financing; each tower's stack retires its share of the land loan.
  4. Sunray funds the wellness-stay suites ($27.3M); Tower C follows once rezoned, sold through presales.
  5. At Year 10, refinancing repays BCH's mezzanine and the Fund; the sponsors keep the buildings.

04 · If it runs leaner

At 65% expenses and 90% occupancy, NOI is $9.7M, coverage 1.87x, and the Year-10 refinance still repays BCH and the Fund with $9.8M to spare.

05 · Why BCH and the Fund back it
  • Mirrors CMHC's MLI Select retirement-home financing — the same route as Heal House Listowel — with BCH's mezzanine repaid in full at Year 10.
  • Seniors' housing and health care in one building, with 83 homes affordable for 40 years.
  • Modern construction and building technology: Cube Building Systems, with construction management by V1 — Mike McGrath, formerly of Tucker HiRise.
  • A pension partner alongside: LiUNA, through Nesting Ground's guarantee pool.
06 · Stated openly
  • Licensed retirement home under Ontario's Retirement Homes Act, like Heal House Listowel; an experienced operator or management contract until Heal House has a track record.
  • Rents of $6,000 / $7,800 all-in need a Markham market study. Earlier Listowel/Barrie figures (~$14,000/room) are well above market and are not used.
  • The Fund's 4% return and Year-10 exit are placeholders; CMHC's retirement-home route to be confirmed.
