03 · How the land gets bought
  1. LiUNA pension invests in Nesting Ground, building its Affordable Housing Guarantee Pool.
  2. The guarantee lifts the land loan from $19.5M (65% of value) to the full $31.0M price — a $11.5M top-up guarantee.
  3. We buy from the receiver and pay out the secured lenders (~$30.3M owing).
  4. At construction close, the Common Ground stack pays the $18.0M Day-1 land draw against the loan; the raise retires the remaining $13.0M.
  5. Nesting Ground owns and operates the rental; Heal House runs the programs; LiUNA builds.

06 · Stated openly
  • Assumes MLI Select and BCH fund the short-stay suites. CMHC normally excludes short-stay space; if it holds that line, the suites need separate cash (e.g. Sunray). The podium is resident amenity space, which CMHC normally accepts.
  • 55+ must stay independent rental. Two bundled care services trigger Ontario's Retirement Homes Act and move CMHC to a lower-leverage product.
  • BCH contribution is $354K per affordable home — high; phasing Tower A first lowers the first ask.

05 · What it does to the building

Giving Heal House the podium rent-free and carrying its operating cost moves Year-1 coverage from 1.81x (leased) to 1.69x; Year-10 refinance coverage is 2.05x. Short-stay and rental income fund the podium. The building stays well above lender minimums.

06 · Stated openly
  • 85% uptake across all homes is a sponsor assumption. Seniors at that level is credible; families in Tower A are more likely 15–30%.
  • Membership is optional and separate from the lease. If mandatory, CMHC and BCH count it as rent; bundled care services trigger the Retirement Homes Act.
  • $350 is about 25% of an affordable 1-bed rent. Offer a subsidized tier for the 287 affordable homes.
  • Revenue multiples above 2–3x rely on the HealConnect platform and insurer revenue being real; lead with EBITDA.
  • Common Ground homes are owned by the non-profit and carry affordability covenants; a saleable portfolio needs market seniors' buildings held outside the structure.
