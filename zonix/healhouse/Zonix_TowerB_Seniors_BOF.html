03 · How the site comes together
  1. LiUNA's pension backs Nesting Ground's guarantee pool, which lifts the land loan to the full $31.0M price.
  2. The site is bought from the receiver; Tower B carries $17.0M of the all-in land cost by floor area.
  3. Tower A proceeds separately as rental with its own partner; 80 family & recovery suites ($27.3M) are funded outside this stack (Sunray).
  4. At construction close, MLI Select, the Fund, BCH and sponsor equity retire the land loan and fund the build.
04 · The honest read

At today's condo-rental comps, Tower B is worth $166.8M against a $196.7M cost — a $29.9M gap. Independent living with Heal House services typically rents above plain condo rentals. Here is what a premium does:

05 · Why the Building Ontario Fund
  • Patient capital makes the 40-year covenant viable for the 83 affordable homes.
  • Pension partner alongside: LiUNA, through Nesting Ground's guarantee pool.
  • Shovel-ready land: zoning approved, priced through a court process, transit at Yonge & Steeles.
  • Fits the program: 411 of the ~2,000 homes, at the program's $75.5K per home.
06 · Stated openly
  • The Fund invests on commercial terms; its instrument and return are to be set with the Fund.
  • Independent living only — bundling two care services triggers Ontario's Retirement Homes Act.
  • Development charges ($19.5M) are estimates, with affordable homes assumed exempt.
  • Approvals, stamped drawings, final GFA and the Tower B floor-area split are unverified.
  • Tower A's partner must fund its share of the land at closing.
