01 — The verdict

It works — because the capital is patient.

On these rents the building is worth about $484,240,562 — relative to its $463.9M cost. The deal pencils because BCH's split tranche (a $101,126,870 contribution vesting against the deeply affordable homes, plus a $84,427,020 mezz that is payment-free until Year 10 and repaid with interest by Year 20), the City contribution and the landowner's donated land value fund the gap rents alone can't finance. Year-10 supplemental DSCR 2.24×.
