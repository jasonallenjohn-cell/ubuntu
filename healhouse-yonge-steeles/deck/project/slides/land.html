09 · Buying the land

From receivership to construction in four steps

1

Guarantee

LiUNA’s pension backs Nesting Ground’s guarantee pool: an $11.5M guarantee lifts the land loan from $19.5M to the full $31.0M.

2

Purchase

Heal House buys from the court-appointed receiver. $8.7M of equity covers transfer tax, carry and pre-development.

3

Build

Towers A and B close construction financing; each tower’s stack retires its share of the land loan.

4

Year 10

Refinancing repays every BCH dollar. The Fund stays to Year 20. Heal House keeps the buildings.

Heal House at Yonge & Steeles · Bid ~$31.0M; all-in land cost $35.7M with closing and 18 months of carry

A firm offer with few conditions, backed by the guarantee, is what wins in receivership.
